Founder's Story · Rohit Addanki

From artist to entrepreneur

Lead actor, OTT deal-maker and co-producer — a journey of determination and growth that built this company.

Rohit steadily carved his path through the Telugu entertainment industry. From supporting beginnings to lead roles in popular serials — Attarintiki Daaredi, Devatha and Rangula Ratnam — he proved his screen presence across every format. He went on to feature in Major, Ghost and the N.T.R. Biopic, alongside the acclaimed web series Three Roses.

Expanding beyond acting, Rohit stepped into co-production, OTT acquisitions, dubbing rights and syndication — selling close to 17 Telugu films into Hindi and North Indian language markets. From artist to entrepreneur, he continues to grow as a dynamic force in cinema.
