How to get a Google Drive direct link

  1. Upload your video to Google Drive.
  2. Right-click the file → Share → change to "Anyone with the link" → Copy link.
  3. You'll get a URL like: https://drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID/view?usp=sharing
  4. Paste that full URL in the field below. Done.

Or: YouTube (recommended for trailers)

  1. Upload to YouTube → set visibility to Unlisted (so only people with the link can see it).
  2. Copy the video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
  3. Paste it below — the developer will embed it as a player on the site.
